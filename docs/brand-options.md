# Branding exploration — no selection

Three proposed directions were compared against sibling brand guides on 2026-10-13.
BundleDock uses stacked shipping packages; WorkHarbor uses an anchor; CrewBook uses
a star/book. All share navy/teal, geometric marks and outlined Bricolage Grotesque
plus IBM Plex Sans. SheetMill should share precision without duplicating a mark.
No sibling artwork or font files have been copied and no SheetMill logo is adopted.

| Concept | Palette | Mark proposal | Assessment | Rating |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — Teal & Copper | Ink `#173A46`, teal `#197E83`, mint `#60D8C3`, copper `#D78A54`, paper `#F3F0E8` | Aligned reference-card stack around a simple mill axle | Warm craftsmanship and family coherence; teal is less distinct from siblings | 8/10 |
| B — Indigo & Amber | Night `#242B49`, indigo `#555AA8`, periwinkle `#B4B8F2`, amber `#E2A64B`, mist `#F4F5FA` | One folded sheet with three distinct output layers | Best editorial fit and clear family differentiation; recommend exploring first | 9/10 |
| C — Plum & Lime | Deep `#35243F`, plum `#7C4B84`, orchid `#D1A7D7`, lime `#C1D966`, cream `#FBF8F1` | Three output ribbons through a reference grid | Most distinctive; grid/ribbons need simplification at favicon size | 8.5/10 |

Ratings are design judgments, not measured user preference. Recommendation B does
not select the final palette; a human choice and actual candidate previews follow.

## Measured text contrast

Calculated from sRGB relative luminance using `(Llighter + .05)/(Ldarker + .05)`.
These are color-pair measurements, not an accessibility certification of a logo.

| Pair | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark ink on light paper | 10.66:1 | 12.72:1 | 13.42:1 |
| Main accent on light paper | 4.24:1 | 5.65:1 | 6.19:1 |
| Bright accent on dark ink | 7.00:1 | 7.30:1 | 9.07:1 |

A's teal-on-paper pair misses the 4.5:1 normal-text target; use ink for body text
or darken that text accent in a candidate. Copper/amber/orchid decoration must be
checked in context before text use. No 16/32/128 px or monochrome logo testing
has occurred because no candidate logo assets were authorized/generated yet.
